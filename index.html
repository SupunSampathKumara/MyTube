Frequently Asked Questions

Is MyTube free to use?

Yes, MyTube is 100% free and open-source. There are no registration forms, subscriptions, or hidden charges. We built it as a premium, ad-free alternative for clean media retrieval.

How does MyTube operate without running an expensive downloader server?

MyTube leverages static client-side rendering optimized for GitHub Pages. It analyzes links directly in the browser and provides a dual fallback system: secure redirection to ad-free download streams and a high-fidelity simulation engine to fetch and demo video assets.

Can I extract MP3 audio from a video?

Absolutely! The format selector allows you to choose between Video (MP4) and Audio (MP3) formats before extraction, allowing you to save high-fidelity music and podcasts directly.

Why should I use the MyTube Bookmarklet?

The Bookmarklet is the easiest way to download videos. Instead of copying URLs, opening our site, and pasting them, you simply click the bookmark while viewing a video, and it loads the video details instantly here.
